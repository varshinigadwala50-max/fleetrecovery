Robot Fleet Recovery Under Cascading Failures

A simulated intelligent recovery system that detects robot failures, identifies cascading effects and automatically restores fleet operations.

What is a Cascading Failure?

A cascading failure occurs when one robot fails and its failure affects other robots or connected systems. This can gradually reduce the performance of the entire robot fleet.
